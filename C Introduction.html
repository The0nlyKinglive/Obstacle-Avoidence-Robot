INTRODUCTION
Hello everyone! Welcome to our project demonstration. Today we are going to show you our Autonomous Obstacle Avoidance Robot using Quarky.
2
Have you ever seen a robotic vacuum cleaner at home? Or heard about self-driving cars?
How can they move around without hitting objects? This is exactly what we tried to understand and build through this project.
3
Once we switch it on, it starts moving independently.
If something comes in front of it, it does not crash — instead, it stops, checks both sides, and chooses a clear path. Our project works on the same principle — we built this robot to understand how modern robots and self-driving systems work in real life.
4
In simple words, this robot can "see", "think", and "decide" where to go.
And the most interesting part is — once we switch it on, it does everything by itself without any human help or remote control.
5
Even though this is a small model, the technology behind it is very powerful.
It is used in many real-world applications. This project helps us understand how robots in the real world are designed to move safely and intelligently in complex environments.
