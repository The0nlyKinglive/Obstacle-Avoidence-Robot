2. AIM

 project goal
The main aim of this project is to develop an autonomous and intelligent robotic system that can move safely, make correct decisions, and function reliably in different and changing environments.
In simple words, we wanted to build a robot that can observe its surroundings, think logically, and make safe decisions without any human control.
⚙️ This project focuses on important objectives such as:
1
Sensor integrity
ensuring the sensor gives accurate and consistent readings so the robot does not make wrong decisions.
2
Real-time processing
allowing the robot to quickly understand the situation and respond immediately.
3
Adaptive navigation
enabling the robot to change its direction based on obstacles in its path.
4
Environmental awareness
helping the robot stay aware of objects and people around it.
5
Collision prevention system
making sure the robot avoids hitting obstacles and moves safely.
6
System reliability and stability
ensuring the robot works smoothly and continuously without errors.
7
Logical algorithm-based decision making
allowing the robot to follow programmed reasoning instead of random movement.
8
Human-safe design
The robot should operate safely near people.
9
Scalability concept
The system should be expandable for future improvements.
10
Precision control
The robot should move accurately and smoothly.
So overall, our aim was not just to build a simple moving machine, but to create a smart, responsive, and safe system that represents the foundation of modern intelligent robotics.
This small model demonstrates how future technologies like autonomous vehicles and service robots are designed to operate independently in complex real-world environments.
