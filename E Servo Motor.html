What is a Servo Motor?

Think of it like the robot's neck! It can turn to specific positions very precisely. In our robot, it rotates the ultrasonic sensor left and right so the robot can look around!

For Beginners: Think of it like this...

🦒 Just like a giraffe moves its neck to look around,
🔄 This motor rotates exactly where we tell it to,
👀 It helps the robot's eyes (sensor) look left and right!

What does it do?

  • POSITIONS - Moves to exact angles
  • ROTATES - Turns left and right precisely
  • HOLDS - Stays in position even when pushed
  • SCANS - Moves the sensor to look around
  • POINTS - Can point at specific directions
