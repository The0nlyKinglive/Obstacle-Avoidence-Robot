What are DC Motors?

Think of them as the robot's legs! They spin the wheels to make the robot move forward, backward, and turn. Without them, the robot would just sit in one place!

For Beginners: Think of it like this...

🦵 Just like your legs help you walk and run,
🔄 These motors spin the wheels to move,
🏃 They can go forward, backward, and turn!

What do they do?

  • MOVE FORWARD - Spin wheels to go ahead
  • MOVE BACKWARD - Reverse direction
  • TURN LEFT - One motor slows down
  • TURN RIGHT - Other motor slows down
  • STOP - Hold the robot in place
