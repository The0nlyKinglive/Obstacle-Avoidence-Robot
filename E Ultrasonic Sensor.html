What is an Ultrasonic Sensor?

Think of it like the robot's eyes! Just like bats use sound to see in the dark, this sensor uses sound waves to detect obstacles. It sends out a sound that humans can't hear and listens for the echo to come back!

For Beginners: Think of it like this...

🦇 Just like a bat shouts and listens for echoes to find food,
👁️ This sensor sends out "click" sounds and waits for them to bounce back,
📏 It measures how long the sound takes to return to know how far away things are!

What does it do?

  • SENDS - Sends out high-frequency sound waves
  • LISTENS - Waits for the echo to come back
  • CALCULATES - Measures the time taken for echo
  • MEASURES - Tells us how far away things are
  • WARNS - Alerts when something is too close
