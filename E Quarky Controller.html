What is Quarky?

Quarky is like the brain of our robot! Just like your brain tells your body what to do, Quarky tells the robot when to move, turn, or stop. It's a small computer that thinks and makes decisions!

For Beginners: Think of it like this...

🤔 Imagine Quarky as the robot's brain:
👁️ It gets information from the robot's eyes (ultrasonic sensor)
🧠 It thinks about what to do (processing)
🦵 It tells the robot's legs (motors) how to move!

What does Quarky do?

  • SEES - Connects to sensors to detect obstacles
  • THINKS - Processes information quickly
  • DECIDES - Chooses which way to go
  • CONTROLS - Tells motors when to move
  • CONNECTS - Can talk to phones and WiFi
